# Terminology — ЧтоХочу Domain Glossary

This document defines the vocabulary used across product, architecture, API, and code. Terms are capitalized when referring to the domain concept; lowercase when referring to the generic word.

---

## User

A person who has registered an account on the platform. A User is authenticated via email/password or OAuth (VK, Yandex). A User owns wishlists, shopping lists, friendships, and notifications. Every User has exactly one Profile.

## Profile

The public-facing identity of a User. Contains: username (unique handle), display name, avatar, bio, birthday (with privacy controls). Other users discover and reference a User through their Profile. The Profile is editable only by its owner.

## Wish

A single item that a User wants. A Wish belongs to exactly one Wishlist. A Wish carries: title, optional product URL, optional image, optional price, a necessity level (e.g. "nice to have", "really want", "must have"), optional notes, and social metadata (likes, comments, claim status). A Wish can be in the Inbox (unassigned to a specific wishlist) as a transient state.

## Wishlist

A named, ordered collection of Wishes owned by a User. A Wishlist has a title, optional description, an emoji icon, and a visibility tier (personal, link, public). A Wishlist can have Participants — other Users invited to collaboratively add and manage Wishes within it. The owner can set per-participant permissions.

## ShoppingList

A named collection of ShoppingListItems shared among Participants for collaborative real-time shopping. Unlike a Wishlist (which represents things you *want*), a ShoppingList represents things you need to *buy now*. A ShoppingList has an owner, a set of Participants, and tracks completion progress (how many items are checked off).

## ShoppingListItem

A single entry in a ShoppingList. A ShoppingListItem carries: text (the item name), a checked/purchased flag, optional quantity, and the identity of the user who checked it (if checked). ShoppingListItems are the reference domain for collaborative editing — they support concurrent add, edit, check/uncheck, and delete operations by multiple participants in real time and offline.

## Friend

A User with whom another User has an established, mutually accepted Friendship. Friendship is bidirectional — if A is friends with B, then B is friends with A. Friends can see each other's public wishlists and interact socially (likes, comments, claims).

## Friendship

The relationship entity between two Users. A Friendship has a lifecycle: `pending` (one user sent a request) → `accepted` (both users agreed) or `declined`/`cancelled`. The Friendship record tracks who initiated the request and when it was accepted.

## Participant

A User who has been invited to and joined a Wishlist or ShoppingList as a collaborator. A Participant has a role and a set of permissions within the shared entity (e.g. can add wishes, can manage participants, can edit list settings). The owner of a Wishlist or ShoppingList is always a Participant with full permissions.

## Share

The act or mechanism of making a Wishlist, ShoppingList, or Wish accessible to others. Sharing can be: via a ShareToken (link sharing), via friend invitation (participant sharing), or via public visibility (discovery). A Share produces a deep link that resolves through the SharePreviewPage or the public web.

## ShareToken

An opaque, unguessable string that grants access to a specific shared entity (Wishlist, ShoppingList, or Wish) via a deep link. A ShareToken is generated by the owner and can be revoked. The deep link `https://app.chtohochu.ru/s/{token}` resolves to a preview page where the recipient can view the entity and, if eligible, join as a Participant.

## Invite

A request to join a shared entity, or a request to become a Friend. An Invite has a sender, a recipient, a target entity (Wishlist, ShoppingList, or "friendship"), and a status (`pending`, `accepted`, `declined`, `expired`). Invites can be delivered in-app (via the Events/Notifications feed) or via an external share link.

## Claim

A declaration by a User (not the Wishlist owner) that they intend to fulfill a specific Wish — typically by purchasing it as a gift. A Claim reserves the Wish so other viewers know it is spoken for. The identity of the claimer is **hidden from the Wishlist owner** to preserve gift surprise, but is visible to other viewers. A Claim can be released (cancelled by the claimer). A claimed Wish that has been purchased can be marked as **fulfilled**.

## Notification

A system-generated message informing a User about a relevant event: friend request received, friend request accepted, invited to a wishlist, new wish added to a shared wishlist, wish claimed, mention, etc. Notifications are delivered via three channels: in-app (Events feed), realtime (WebSocket push to active clients), and push (FCM to mobile devices). Notifications carry identifiers and event metadata, not authoritative domain state.

## Event

An entry in the in-app activity feed (the Events tab). An Event is a persisted, user-facing record of something that happened in the user's social graph: "Misha added a wish to Birthday 2026", "Lena accepted your friend request". Events have read/unread state, type-specific icons, and deep-link navigation to the relevant entity. Events are the persisted counterpart of Notifications.

## Seller

A merchant account on the platform. A Seller manages a Catalog of Products and may publish Offers. The Seller surface (seller cabinet at a dedicated subdomain) is a future domain — architecturally provisioned but out of scope for the initial release. A Seller is distinct from a regular User; sellers authenticate through a separate flow and have different permissions.

## Product

A catalog entity representing a sellable item, managed by a Seller. A Product has a title, description, images, category, and a default price. In the future, a Wish may be linked to a Product (instead of or in addition to a freeform URL), enabling price tracking, offer matching, and recommendations. Products are out of scope for the initial release.

## Catalog

The structured collection of Products published by Sellers. The Catalog supports browsing, search, and category filtering. In the future, the Catalog powers product-linked wishes and offer matching. The Catalog is out of scope for the initial release.

## Offer

A time-bound commercial proposition from a Seller for a specific Product — a price, a discount, availability, or a promotional deal. Offers could in the future be matched against Wishes to notify users when something they want goes on sale. Offers are out of scope for the initial release.
